If the first thing you meet each morning is a screen, you are handing the tone of
your whole day to whatever is on it — a message, a memory, a notification from
someone you are trying to heal from.

## Do this

1. Before you pick up your phone, step outside. A porch, a doorway, a window
   thrown open all count.
2. Stay for ten minutes. You do not have to do anything while you are there.
3. Let the light hit your face and your eyes — no sunglasses, and never look
   straight at the sun.
4. If ten minutes is too long, start with two. Two is not a failure.

## Making it stick

Pair it with something you already do. Coffee on the step instead of at the
counter. The dog out five minutes earlier. Anything you already do every day will
carry this along with it.

## Why it works

Morning light sets the clock that decides when you get sleepy at night. People
coming out of long stress often have that clock badly out of order — wired late,
exhausted at dawn. Light in the first hour after waking is the strongest, simplest
correction there is, and it is free.
