You know the moment. Something happens — a message, a smell, a tone of voice —
and suddenly you are not entirely here anymore. Your chest goes tight, or
everything goes flat and far away.

This practice brings you back into the room. It takes two minutes and no one
around you has to know you are doing it.

## Do this

Out loud if you can, silently if you cannot:

- **Five** things you can see. Say what they are. "Blue mug. Chipped tile."
- **Four** things you can feel. The chair. The floor. Your sleeve. The air.
- **Three** things you can hear. Traffic. A fan. Your own breathing.
- **Two** things you can smell.
- **One** thing you can taste.

Then one slow breath: in for four, out for six.

## Afterward

Do not judge how it went. The only thing this practice is for is putting you back
in the present moment. If you got halfway through and felt steadier, it worked.

If this is happening often, that is worth telling someone — a trusted friend, a
therapist, or in one of the group sessions. Carrying it alone is the part that
wears people out.
